Baronial Asset Request Form & Storage Terms

Storage Location:

Olympic Crown Storage
915 Bendix Drive, Salisbury NC 28146

To request Baronial Assets for your event or function, please fill out the bulk PDF form. Once complete, download the PDF to your device and email the form to quartermaster@sacredstone.atlantia.sca.org and exchequer@sacredstone.atlantia.sca.org.

Arrangements will be made to pick up the items at Baronial storage. The Quartermaster and Exchequer will print the form for the requester to physically sign at check-out and take pictures. The signed form will be scanned with the images attached and saved for reference, and a copy of both will be emailed to the requester.

After the event is over, the requester arranges with the Quartermaster or Exchequer to return the items to Baronial storage no later than 14 days after the event. The Quartermaster and Exchequer will note on the form when the items were returned, take pictures, scan the updated form, attach the after-pictures, and send a copy to the requester.

Notice on Request Approvals:

Submitting a checkout request through this portal logs your inquiry for Quartermaster and Exchequer review, but does not guarantee or constitute immediate asset issuance. All gear allocations—especially restricted officer regalia and high-value baronial assets—remain subject to formal review, officer verification, and written approval in accordance with Baronial Policy.

Terms & Conditions

  1. Items borrowed are the responsibility of the individual named above. It is the sponsoring branch's responsibility to repair or replace any items which are broken, lost or stolen.
  2. Borrower will arrange a time to access the storage unit with a key holder, no later than ten days prior to when the items are needed and at the key holder's convenience. (Don't ask for access to pick up items on the morning of an event.)
  3. Borrower will arrange a time to return items to the storage unit with a key holder. Borrowed items will be returned to the storage unit no later than 14 days after the end of the event for which they were borrowed. Items will not be 'handed off' to another person for use at another event. *

Status & Condition Definitions

Status Definitions

  • Storage (Deep Green): In Unit — Stored properly in primary storage.
  • Non-Unit (Light Green): Cannot be stored in unit, but not presently signed out.
  • Elkin Site (Light Green): Stored at the Ekin site, but not specifically signed out.
  • Signed Out (Yellow): Signed out — for event or for an officer term.
  • Missing (Bright Red): Missing — Location unknown.

Condition Definitions

  • Excellent (Deep Green): Pristine condition.
  • Good (Light Green): Minor wear, fully functional.
  • Worn / Unfinished (Yellow): Shows age or incomplete/unfinished but still usable.
  • Flawed (Bright Pink): Damaged, incomplete or unfinished; may become unusable.
  • Unusable (Bright Red): Damaged, broken or incomplete; cannot be used until fixed.
