A Season of Charity and Harvest

A Warm Welcome

The Canton of Salesberie Glen invites you to join us in a friendly harvest festival. We welcome everyone into a warm hall to share light, friendship, and a day of relaxation before the busy holiday season kicks off.

As the year wanes and the fields lie fallow, the folk of our Barony prepare to observe the Feast of St. Martin of Tours. It is a time marked by the cooling of the hearth and the gathering of kin, when we look back upon the bounty of the harvest and forward to the quietude of winter.

Of the Holiday and its Observance

St. Martin, soldier and servant, remains a beacon of humility for all who dwell within these borders. His act of dividing his own cloak to shield a freezing beggar serves as our charge for the coming month: to seek the needs of our neighbors and to practice charity in all our dealings.

Historically, this day—the eleventh of the eleventh month—marks the threshold of the Quadragesima Sancti Martini, the season of restraint and reflection. We invite all to join in the quiet traditions of the season:

  • The Lighting of Lanterns: Reflecting the illumination of the path during the darkest nights.
  • The Feasting of the Goose: A celebration of the final harvest before the winter stores are sealed.
  • The Settling of Accounts: Ensuring all debts, both material and social, are reconciled before the closing of the year.
