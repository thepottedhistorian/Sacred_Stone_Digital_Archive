Martial Activities

Pas de l'Oie Criarde

Tournament of the Clamoring Goose

Let it be known to all knights, men-at-arms, and gentles of good will, that upon the twenty-first day of November, Anno Societatis LXI, the Canton of Salesberie Glen, in the Barony of the Sacred Stone, shall hold Le Pas de l'Oie Criarde, in celebration of St. Martin of Tours — that good soldier whose hiding place, legend says, was cried aloud by geese, and who has been honored ever since with feast, mirth, and a well-roasted bird.

Combat & Field

Armored and rapier combatants who would take the field are asked to seek counsel of Lord Giovanni DeMoncillis and Noble Ellie Fire Tender, who shall speak to the day's combat.

  • Heavy Combat: Pas de l'Oie Criarde (Tournament of the Clamoring Goose)
  • Rapier Combat: Pas de l'Oie Criarde (Tournament of the Clamoring Goose)
  • Archery: Details pending.

Arts & Sciences Note

Those of gentler and quieter talents are asked to seek out Toth Eva Franciska, who shall serve as the day's Minister of Arts & Sciences, and who welcomes all who would bring work, word, or wonder to the populace.

Marshal Information

Questions regarding field safety or authorization may be directed to our Marshal in Charge. Please ensure all kit is inspected and up to date with Kingdom standards before stepping onto the field.
