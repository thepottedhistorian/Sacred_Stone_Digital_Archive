The Feast of St. Martin

In the sixteenth century, the Martinimahl was a final, joyous gathering of the guild and the hearth before the winter's turning. As the fields are cleared and the grain is safely stored, our community gathers to break bread in a spirit of thanksgiving and fellowship.

The Evening’s Fare

We are currently finalizing a 16th-century German feast menu. Please check back soon for further details and the expected offerings for the table.

Note: There will be no day board provided. However, to keep our guests warm and refreshed, coffee, tea, and water will be available in the hall throughout the day.
