Baronial Championships
The Baronial Arts & Sciences Champion Competition
The Phoenix Nest: Add a Little Spice

Like the Phoenix of old, artisans will gather spices and use them to create something new. Whether you set them aflame or steep them in water, at least one spice (such as cinnamon, cardamom, saffron, etc.) must be the central focus of your entry for the Sacred Stone Baronial Arts & Sciences Champion Competition.

Inspirational Entry Examples:
  • A tasty cinnamon bread
  • Spun fiber dyed with turmeric
  • Paint pigments created with saffron

Documentation Guidelines:

Documentation is key for this competition and should reflect pre-17th century products, methods, and materials where possible. Citations should be included with at least one reference to the historical inspiration. An executive summary is recommended for documentation more than three pages.

Whether you are making food, dyeing fiber, or creating pigments, we look forward to seeing what new form your spices take!

Judging details will be provided after consultation with the incoming baronage. For questions regarding the competition or the A&S Champion role, please contact Lady Margot Leclerc at margotleclersca@gmail.com.

Baronial Championships
Baronial Beekeeper Championship Contest
Ancient Embers & The Gilded Hive

All competitors must be current, active beekeepers managing their own honeybee colonies. This ensures the Barony maintains a living connection to the craft, keeping our tables supplied with local honey and our courts bright with traditional beeswax.

The Continuity Pledge: The Triple-Jar Requirement

Entrants must provide their honey harvest in three distinct vessels. This act symbolizes the renewal of the Barony and the beekeeper's pledge of ongoing service:

  • The Judging Jar (The "Sun-Drenched" Terroir): A technical assessment of your spring or summer harvest, celebrating the capture of raw sunlight within a jar. Entrants should present at least 2 oz of their lightest honey in a clear, unadorned glass vessel for color, clarity, and taste evaluation.
  • The Ember Jar: One jar (size at the entrant's discretion) gifted to the Outgoing Baronage to honor the light of their past leadership.
  • The Renewal Jar: One jar (size at the entrant's discretion) gifted to the Incoming Baronage to serve as fuel for the light of the future.
Your Apiary Story

Along with your honey, please include a short, written companion to introduce us to your bees. There is no page limit; please feel free to share as much or as little of your story as you like, covering these two areas:

  • Your Nectar Flow & Flora: A brief look at your local forage and how the seasonal honey flow shaped the taste and clarity of your judging jar.
  • The Apiary Chronicle: A personal history of how you got into beekeeping and the story behind your current hives.

Baronial Armored Warlord Tournament
The Muster of Shield and Steel

To determine who possesses the tactical brilliance and martial prowess required to lead our powerful vanguard into battle, a grand tournament shall be contested on the auspicious occasion of our glorious Barony’s Birthday.

It is my desire to witness the strength of unity and strategic fellowship. Therefore, this championship track will be fought as a Three-Fighter Team Tournament. The field will be met in an exhausting Round Robin format, testing the endurance and coordination of every alliance present.

When the dust of the lists settles, the martial leader of the ultimate victorious team shall be named and recognized as the rightful Warlord of the Sacred Stone.

In service and honor,
Achbar

The Trial of Precision & Spirit

Martial Fields
Baronial Thrown Weapons Championship
The Trial of Flight & Steel

Call to the marksmen, throwers, and scouts of the Sacred Stone! The hour has arrived to test your precision, steady your hand, and determine who will step forward as the Barony's next sovereign protector of the targets.

This year's competition will try your mastery across different distances and traditional implements to find the thrower with the truest aim. The parameters of the championship list are declared as follows:

  • The Format: The tournament will be contested as a formal Royal Round.
  • The Arsenal: Competitors will throw both knives and axes.
  • The Marks: Target lines will be set cleanly at distances of both 10 feet and 20 feet.
  • Loaner Gear Available: For those traveling light or wishing to try their skill, loaner weapons will be fully available at the range.

From master throwers to those picking up an axe for the first time, all are welcome to step up to the line, test their aim, and compete for the honor of the Barony!

Baronial Rapier Championship
The Duel of Wit & Steel

To the swiftest blades, cunning tacticians, and dedicated duelists of the Sacred Stone! The call is issued for all who aspire to serve the Barony as Their Excellencies' next sovereign defender of the white scarf and steel.

This year's field will demand endurance, adaptability, and unwavering honor as you test your defense against every challenger in the list. The parameters of the championship tournament are declared as follows:

  • The Ultimate Honor: Competitors will fight for the distinguished privilege of serving as the Baronial Rapier Champion to Their Excellencies of the Sacred Stone.
  • The Tournament Format: The crown will be contested in a grueling and thorough Round Robin tournament, ensuring every blade meets face-to-face on the field.
  • The Standard of Entry: All combatants must be fully authorized and field-ready according to Kingdom standards at the time of the list's opening.

From master duelists to those stepping into their very first championship list, all are welcome to take the field, test their steel, and compete for the honor of the Barony!

Sovereign Performance Track
Baronial Bardic Championship

"Hearken, good gentles of Sacred Stone! I, Lord Gawain de Barri, call for those who would seek the mantle of Bardic Champion."

To step forward into this noble office, candidates must submit a formal Letter of Intent and prepare three distinct performance works tailored to the pillars of our populace:

  • The First Work: Crafted especially for our youth.
  • The Second Work: Intended to inspire newcomers to the Dream.
  • The Third Work: Honoring the experiences of those who have walked these roads for many years.

Through story, song, poetry, or other bardic arts, show how you would serve the people of Sacred Stone as their Champion.

Details on where to send your letters of intent are being finalized. Check back soon for submission paths—we cannot wait to hear your stories and songs!
